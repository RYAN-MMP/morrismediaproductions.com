03:00Background

Ryan Morris spent 25 years in network television, starting in 1999 on live remotes and ending as a breaking-news editor at CBS — cutting national interrupts and morning-news SOTs and VOs. In 2022 he received a News & Documentary Emmy as an editor on CBS Mornings.

He founded Morris Media Productions in 2024 to bring that speed and that eye for story to brands, producers, and independent projects.
